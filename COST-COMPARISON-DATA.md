## 9. WHATSAPP BUSINESS API

### Pricing structure (as of July 2025 — per-message model)

**Layer 1: Meta base fees (vary by country + template type)**
| Template type | Approximate fee |
|---------------|----------------|
| Marketing template | $0.0149–$0.0499/message (US numbers) |
| Utility template (within 24h session) | Free (from 2025) |
| Authentication template | $0.0034–$0.0099/message |

**Layer 2: BSP markup (example: Twilio)**
| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Twilio per-message fee | +$0.005 (inbound or outbound) |
| Total with Meta fee (marketing) | ~$0.0199–$0.0549/message |

**Italian/European market note:** Meta pricing is country-code based. EU rates differ from US rates — verify at business.whatsapp.com/rates.

**Volume-based math for SMBs:**
- 1,000 marketing messages/mo: ~$20–$55/mo (Meta + Twilio)
- 5,000 marketing messages/mo: ~$100–$275/mo
- Utility messages (within session): effectively free from July 2025

**Alternative BSPs:** MessageBird, 360dialog, Vonage — all add markup over Meta base rates, typically $0.003–$0.008/message.
