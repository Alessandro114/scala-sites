**Purpose:** Benchmark for SCALA vertical site builds — hero types, palettes, micro-interactions, mobile patterns
**Sources:** Awwwards, Orpetron Awards, CSS Design Awards, FWA, Elias Studio top-30 lists, direct site audits

---

## How to Read This File

Each entry documents:
- **URL** — verified (live at research time) or marked "da verificare"
- **Hero type** — what dominates above the fold
- **Palette** — 2-4 dominant colors
- **Signature interaction** — the one move that makes it memorable
- **Mobile approach** — how the experience adapts
- **Why it wins** — the single sentence that matters for replication

## 1. Restaurant / Fine Dining

### 1.1 Ultraviolet by Paul Pairet
- **URL:** https://uvbypp.cc
- **Hero:** Full-screen experimental video with ambient sound cues; multi-chapter scrolling narrative structure
- **Palette:** Black dominant, electric neon accents (the restaurant itself uses 60 LED RGB projectors)
- **Signature interaction:** Sound plays automatically — the site is treated as a multi-sensory experience, not just visual. Sections transition with cinematic cut logic.
- **Mobile:** Graceful degradation to high-resolution stills with audio toggle
- **Why it wins:** The only restaurant site where the website IS the product experience. Visiting the URL is the teaser for a 20-course immersive dinner priced at ~$1,000/head.

### 1.2 Central Restaurante (Lima — World's #1, 2023)
- **URL:** https://centralrestaurante.com.pe/en/
- **Hero:** Editorial full-bleed photography, altitude-journey metaphor (Virgilio Martinez's menu explores Peru from ocean floor to Andean peaks)
- **Palette:** Earth tones — ochre, stone, deep green, cream
- **Signature interaction:** Menu sections load as geographic "elevation zones" — the site mirrors the tasting menu structure
- **Mobile:** Vertical scroll with large typography, tap-to-explore altitude chapters
- **Why it wins:** Structure mirrors content. The IA itself is the storytelling device.

### 1.3 Noma (Copenhagen)
- **URL:** https://noma.dk
- **Hero:** Scandinavian white space — NO hero image. A single seasonal line of text and an immediate reservation link.
- **Palette:** Off-white, raw linen, muted forest green, ink black
- **Signature interaction:** Booking IS the hero. The site strips everything non-essential — a radical act for a 3-Michelin-star restaurant.
- **Mobile:** Identical experience — the restraint scales perfectly
- **Why it wins:** Confidence through absence. Nothing screams "we do not need to convince you" louder than a blank field and a reservation button.

### 1.4 Sublimotion (Ibiza)
- **URL:** https://sublimotionibiza.com
- **Hero:** Cinematic full-viewport video with 3D animated transitions between dining scenes; ambient electronic soundtrack
- **Palette:** Deep purple-black, gold, electric cyan
- **Signature interaction:** 3D scene dissolves between course vignettes; cursor changes shape contextually
- **Mobile:** Video replaced by curated stills with fade transitions
- **Why it wins:** €2,000+/head dinner — the site prices itself through visual excess, which IS the brand promise.

### 1.5 Gucci Osteria (Florence)
- **URL:** https://gucciosteria.com/en/florence
- **Hero:** Fashion-editorial stills — product photography aesthetic applied to food, no video
- **Palette:** House green, cream, gold, black
- **Signature interaction:** Scroll reveals dishes as fashion editorials — full bleed, single item per screen, architectural typography
- **Mobile:** Vertical editorial flow; swipe replaces hover
- **Why it wins:** Positions food as luxury goods. Borrowed design grammar from the fashion industry and applied it to hospitality.

## 2. Real Estate / Luxury Property

### 2.1 111 West 57th Street (New York)
- **URL:** https://111w57.com
- **Hero:** Full-viewport aerial Manhattan photography + gradient overlay; Lenis smooth scroll implementation
- **Palette:** Bronze (#7c7262), off-white (#f0eeea), black, with bronze architectural accent
- **Signature interaction:** Clip-path wipe animations on button interactions; parallax depth on scroll; 24-column grid with serif headlines scaling to 120rem
- **Mobile:** Scroll-snap carousel for residences; touch-optimized with static image fallback
- **Why it wins:** Awwwards Site of the Day (May 2025). Treats a $7.75M-$66M tower like a cultural artifact, not a property listing.

### 2.2 Passalacqua (Lake Como)
- **URL:** https://passalacqua.it (branding: positioner.com/hotel-brand-design/passalacqua)
- **Hero:** Editorial photography of "the building-in-the-making" — raw authenticity over polished CGI
- **Palette:** Warm ivory, terracotta, faded gold, deep lake blue
- **Signature interaction:** Stories start on-site, cut mid-narrative — readers who want the ending receive a physical scented postcard. Phygital storytelling loop.
- **Mobile:** Long-form editorial scroll; strong typography with generous leading
- **Why it wins:** Awwwards Honorable Mention. Invented a new genre: phygital luxury hospitality marketing. The postcard mechanic is the most original CTA in the sector.

### 2.3 Douglas Elliman
- **URL:** https://www.elliman.com
- **Hero:** Cinematic hero with tagline "WHERE DO YOU WANT TO GO?" on high-resolution lifestyle imagery
- **Palette:** Black, white, gold — classical luxury restraint
- **Signature interaction:** Location-aware hero — the imagery and copy shift by detected market region
- **Mobile:** Full-featured property search with map integration; aspirational imagery maintained
- **Why it wins:** Treats property search as lifestyle aspiration, not a database query. Best-in-class for the broker/portal hybrid model.

### 2.4 Sotheby's International Realty
- **URL:** https://www.sothebys.com/en/real-estate
- **Hero:** Museum-gallery aesthetic — white space, full-bleed photography, minimal navigation
- **Palette:** Sotheby's blue (#003566), white, warm grey
- **Signature interaction:** Video property tours embedded as full-screen overlays; editorial "collections" framing (not "listings")
- **Mobile:** Gallery-browse adapted to vertical; video auto-pauses on scroll
- **Why it wins:** Borrowed the auction house's editorial credibility. Properties feel like lots in a Christie's catalogue.

### 2.5 Luxury Presence Portfolio (reference agency)
- **URL:** https://www.luxurypresence.com/best-real-estate-agent-websites/
- **Hero:** Varies by project — split-screen, full-bleed architecture photography, or horizontal scroll gallery
- **Palette:** Each site custom — the agency benchmarks the space
- **Signature interaction:** Horizontal scroll galleries for property portfolios; custom cursor designs per agent brand
- **Mobile:** Swipe-native galleries; sticky booking/inquiry bars
- **Why it wins:** Best reference gallery for the luxury real estate sub-vertical. 50+ documented examples with design breakdowns.

## 3. Hotel / Boutique Hotel

### 3.1 Silena — Your Soulful Hotel (South Tyrol)
- **URL:** https://www.silena.it (via Orpetron award listing)
- **Hero:** Full-screen image with cinematic scroll-triggered transitions (BARBA.js page transitions)
- **Palette:** Warm sand, alpine green, white linen
- **Signature interaction:** Vertical menu navigation; GSAP + Angular scroll animations; every page transition is a scene change, not a load
- **Mobile:** Touch-friendly vertical scroll; animated reveal on content sections
- **Why it wins:** Orpetron Award winner. Blends South Tyrolean and Southeast Asian design language — the site IS a cultural statement.

### 3.2 Casa di Legna (Corsica)
- **URL:** https://casadilegna.com (via Orpetron)
- **Hero:** Parallax immersion — layered photography of Corsican landscape with Locomotive Scroll-powered depth
- **Palette:** Warm ivory, terracotta, sea blue, olive green
- **Signature interaction:** Intro animation sequence; smooth parallax depth creates the sensation of "arriving" at the property
- **Mobile:** Parallax simplified to fade-in reveals; gallery remains full-width
- **Why it wins:** The opening animation earns the "beautiful escape" tagline before a single word is read.

### 3.3 CitizenM
- **URL:** https://www.citizenm.com
- **Hero:** Vibrant lobby imagery — colorful interiors immediately communicate "affordable luxury" positioning; booking widget embedded IN the header
- **Palette:** Signature red, black, white — bold graphic system
- **Signature interaction:** Destination search, dates, and guest count integrated directly into hero — zero friction from arrival to booking intent
- **Mobile:** App-parity booking flow; location-aware check-in integration
- **Why it wins:** Removed the step between "inspired by the hero" and "booking." The booking widget IS the hero.

### 3.4 Ace Hotel
- **URL:** https://acehotel.com
- **Hero:** Cinematic full-screen video + signature stencil typography: "Welcome to Ace Hotel" in bold blocky type
- **Palette:** Off-white, concrete grey, black, occasional bold saturated accents (varies by property)
- **Signature interaction:** Property-by-property microsites with independent visual identities under one navigation shell
- **Mobile:** Video fallback to editorial still; stencil typography maintained at mobile scale
- **Why it wins:** Invented the "creative class hotel" website category. Each property has a distinct character — the nav system unifies without homogenizing.

### 3.5 Sapporo Nishiyama Europe (ramen brand)
- **URL:** via Orpetron: sapporo-nishiyama-europe-gmbh listing
- **Hero:** Dark theme intro animation; Three.js-powered atmospheric visuals; "70 years in business" typography anchor
- **Palette:** Dark charcoal, warm gold, steam-white
- **Signature interaction:** Angular + Three.js generates depth field and particle effects that evoke ramen steam; scroll-triggered timeline reveals brand history
- **Mobile:** Static atmospheric still with text reveal on scroll
- **Why it wins:** Restaurant/hospitality site that uses 3D web tech to create *sensory suggestion* — visitors feel warmth before they see a menu.

## 4. Creative Studio / Agency

### 4.1 Resn (Wellington, New Zealand)
- **URL:** https://resn.co.nz
- **Hero:** WebGL surrealist playground — the site IS a demo of their capabilities. Odd, futuristic, eccentric every pixel.
- **Palette:** No fixed palette — changes per project showcase
- **Signature interaction:** The whole site is non-linear. No funnel — curiosity drives exploration. Portfolio pages are mini-films with animated transitions and dynamic content.
- **Mobile:** Simplified but retains the surrealist character
- **Why it wins:** Awwwards multiple winner. The site is the pitch. You experience their work, you don't read about it.

### 4.2 Active Theory (Los Angeles)
- **URL:** https://activetheory.net
- **Hero:** Dynamic, immersive — colored tubes spawn from mouse/touch movements and are networked with other live users; you can see other visitors' movements
- **Palette:** Dark base with dynamic color per visitor interaction
- **Signature interaction:** Shared cursor network — every visitor's movement creates colored tubes visible to all simultaneous users. Collaborative canvas as hero.
- **Mobile:** Touch-spawned color trails replace cursor trails
- **Why it wins:** Communication Arts Award winner. Turned the homepage into a real-time social installation. First agency site to make "other people are here right now" a design feature.

### 4.3 Obys Agency (Ukraine)
- **URL:** https://obys.agency
- **Hero:** Surreal floating elements, alien interface patterns, typography-led editorial work; unpredictable navigation
- **Palette:** Changes per project — the palette instability IS the aesthetic statement
- **Signature interaction:** Weird sounds, unconventional layout — the site disorients intentionally, demonstrating conceptual courage
- **Mobile:** Reduced to core typographic experience
- **Why it wins:** Awwwards Studio of the Year 2023. Uses the site as medium — a living example of artistic courage and UX mastery.

### 4.4 Locomotive (Montreal)
- **URL:** https://locomotive.ca
- **Hero:** Clean typography, soft micro-animations, subtle scroll effects — polished premium feel
- **Palette:** White, dark charcoal, restrained single accent
- **Signature interaction:** Their own Locomotive Scroll library — buttery smooth scroll with custom easing is the signature interaction (they built the tool used by thousands of other sites)
- **Mobile:** Perfect scroll behavior adapts to touch; the library was built mobile-first
- **Why it wins:** Awwwards Agency of the Year × 6. They eat their own cooking: the site runs on their own scroll library, which is the product they're implicitly selling.

### 4.5 Elias Studio (France)
- **URL:** https://www.elias.studio/en
- **Hero:** Editorial dark theme; animated text reveals; portfolio presented as curated selection rather than scrolling grid
- **Palette:** Near-black, white, warm accent
- **Signature interaction:** Hover on project cards triggers full-screen animated preview; custom cursor morphs into play button
- **Mobile:** Stack layout with tap-to-preview
- **Why it wins:** One of the most referenced French digital studios for hospitality/restaurant web design. Their own site demonstrates the editorial restraint they apply to clients.

## 5. Car Dealership / Automotive

### 5.1 Porsche (reference brand benchmark)
- **URL:** https://www.porsche.com
- **Hero:** Full-page background video of latest model; 360-degree vehicle visualizer; performance data overlaid on cinematic shots
- **Palette:** Black, Porsche red/yellow speed accents, Racing Yellow, Arctic Silver — varies by model
- **Signature interaction:** 3D configurator allows real-time color/trim changes with rendering; virtual showroom mode
- **Mobile:** Video fallback to stills; configurator adapted to pinch-zoom
- **Why it wins:** Best-in-class 3D vehicle configuration. The configurator IS the product demo — customers spend 20+ minutes designing before visiting a dealer.

### 5.2 Automobili Pininfarina
- **URL:** https://www.automobili-pininfarina.com
- **Hero:** Dark cinematic video — slow-motion electrified vehicle in motion; no text for 3 seconds
- **Palette:** Matte black, brushed aluminum, electric teal accent
- **Signature interaction:** Parallax vehicle reveal on scroll — car emerges from darkness as user scrolls past each spec section; sound design plays on desktop
- **Mobile:** Full-screen stills with swipe-through spec cards
- **Why it wins:** Winner of three international design awards (2023-2024). Positions an EV as sculpture — the site is an art installation, not a spec sheet.

### 5.3 Ferrari (design reference)
- **URL:** https://www.ferrari.com
- **Hero:** Split hero — half editorial film still, half performance data; no hero CTA, only "Discover"
- **Palette:** Rosso Corsa, black, white, carbon-fiber grey
- **Signature interaction:** Collection navigation where hovering a model name triggers a full-screen color wash of that car's livery; animation on every micro-state
- **Mobile:** Gesture-based model carousel; Rosso wash preserved on tap
- **Why it wins:** The only car brand site that creates desire without a price, a dealer CTA, or a test drive button. Pure brand gravity.

### 5.4 Ridgeline Chrysler (local dealer — best-in-class SMB)
- **URL:** Reference via CyberOptik 2026 list (exact domain: da verificare)
- **Hero:** Rotating inventory photography with immediate search CTA; financing calculator above the fold
- **Palette:** Brand blue + white; inventory photography at scale
- **Signature interaction:** Real-time inventory filter (make/model/price) integrated into hero without page reload
- **Mobile:** One-thumb scroll to filter inventory; click-to-call pinned
- **Why it wins:** Best practice for the local-dealer tier: removes all friction between hero and "show me what you have in my price range."

### 5.5 Lamborghini (configurator benchmark)
- **URL:** https://www.lamborghini.com
- **Hero:** Full-page cinematic video — track footage, no narration; auto-advances through 3 models
- **Palette:** Verde Mantis, Giallo Orion, Arancio Borealis — the brand's extreme color system IS the palette system
- **Signature interaction:** "Build Your Lamborghini" configurator with photorealistic renders; shares configurations as social cards
- **Mobile:** Configurator rebuilt as touch-native experience; renders at 60fps on current-gen phones
- **Why it wins:** Social-shareable configurator outputs turned every build into a marketing touchpoint. User-generated brand content at scale.

## 7. Gym / CrossFit

### 7.1 Barry's (formerly Barry's Bootcamp)
- **URL:** https://www.barrys.com
- **Hero:** Video background (dark aesthetic); hero content transforms based on user location — location-aware promotional overlays fade the original hero and replace it with a market-specific campaign
- **Palette:** Deep black (#111), Barry's red (#D6001A), white, neutral grey
- **Signature interaction:** Context-detection system creates personalized hero states per city; clip-path polygon wipe transitions; seasonal promo system swaps entire hero including background, copy, imagery — all animated
- **Mobile:** Video fallback to static; reduced triangle decorative elements (80px → 40px); touch-optimized class booking
- **Why it wins:** The location-aware hero transformation is unique at this scale. You land on the same URL as someone in London or NYC but see a different site — personalization without a login.

### 7.2 Equinox
- **URL:** https://www.equinox.com
- **Hero:** Responsive video background (multiple quality tiers: 360w mobile → 1920w desktop); tagline "It's Not Fitness. It's Life"
- **Palette:** Custom "Equinox Sans" typeface; dark backgrounds, white text, luxury photography; spa/sauna imagery equal to gym imagery
- **Signature interaction:** Holistic wellness — spa, pilates, recovery, and classes presented as lifestyle equals, not add-ons; login-conditional hero CTAs
- **Mobile:** Art-directed responsive imagery (different crops per breakpoint); app integration CTA above fold
- **Why it wins:** Repositioned a gym as a luxury lifestyle membership. The site's structure (spa first, then fitness) reflects the brand's actual proposition.

### 7.3 F45 Training
- **URL:** https://f45training.com
- **Hero:** Captivating front-page video of diverse workout programs; dark blue and red energy palette
- **Palette:** Dark navy, F45 red, white, high-contrast photography
- **Signature interaction:** Franchise CTA alongside member CTA — two audiences, one hero, zero conflict; video auto-plays with muted sound
- **Mobile:** Video maintained (short looping clip); dual CTA preserved as stacked buttons
- **Why it wins:** Simultaneously markets to franchisees and end users from a single hero. Best two-audience homepage in the fitness vertical.

### 7.4 CrossFit Perpetua (Dublin — boutique benchmark)
- **URL:** https://crossfitperpetua.com (reference via Wodify list)
- **Hero:** Bold headline + authentic in-gym photography; community-forward copy
- **Palette:** Dark charcoal, orange accent, white, exposed-concrete grey
- **Signature interaction:** Community-first structure — testimonials and social proof appear above class schedules; coach profiles with personality-forward copy
- **Mobile:** Class schedule booking pinned as sticky CTA; Instagram feed integrated
- **Why it wins:** Boutique benchmark for community-as-product positioning. Sells belonging, not equipment.

### 7.5 SoulCycle
- **URL:** https://www.soul-cycle.com
- **Hero:** Clean, modern — energetic brand imagery; dark with candlelit studio aesthetic
- **Palette:** Black, white, canary yellow, soul-fire orange
- **Signature interaction:** Class finder with instructor profile previews; instructor personality is a product differentiator visible from search
- **Mobile:** Class finder is the hero on mobile — the site knows its users are booking on phones
- **Why it wins:** Made the instructor a product, not staff. The instructor-profile-as-hero-element is a differentiator no gym template replicates.

| Full-viewport video hero | Hotel, Gym, Fine Dining, Car, Spa | Video creates immersion that photography cannot — the visitor "arrives" |
| Radical minimalism / silence | Fine Dining (Noma), Photography, Law | Confidence communicated through restraint; luxury brands signal status by NOT trying |
| Search/booking as hero | Coworking, Daycare, HVAC, Cleaning | When the primary action is "find/book," the hero IS the funnel |
| Social proof above fold | Vet, Medical, Cleaning, Accounting | Trust-deficit categories convert on reviews, not aesthetics |
| Editorial / magazine framing | Fashion, Fine Dining, Real Estate | Luxury = aspiration; aspiration requires narrative, not spec |
| Interactive floor plan / configurator | Real Estate, Car, Coworking | Removes "I don't know what I'm getting" — the most universal conversion objection |
| Personalization / location-aware hero | Gym (Barry's), Cleaning, Coworking | Same URL, different experience = personalization at scale |
| Split-audience navigation | Daycare, HVAC, Coworking | When B2B and B2C share a homepage, segment them in the hero or lose both |
| WebGL / 3D immersion | Creative Agency, Photography, Automotive | Technical demonstration = credibility signal in tech-forward categories |
| Before/after slider | Dermatology, Cleaning, Plumbing | The most direct visualization of value in transformation-based services |
